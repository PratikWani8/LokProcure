# Single-Vendor Sole-Sourcing Restriction
Purchase requests that name or imply a single specific vendor without
evidence of a competitive bidding process are considered sole-sourced.
Sole-sourced requests are permitted only when the estimated_budget is
under $10,000, or when urgency is "Critical" and no comparable vendor can
meet the required delivery timeline. All other sole-sourced requests must
be flagged as non-compliant and require written justification before
approval.
