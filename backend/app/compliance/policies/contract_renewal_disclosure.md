# Vendor Contract Auto-Renewal Disclosure Rule
Purchase requests involving a vendor engagement expected to recur (implied
by item_description mentioning terms like "recurring," "subscription,"
"annual," "retainer," or "ongoing") must disclose whether the vendor
contract includes an auto-renewal clause. Recurring-sounding requests that
do not address auto-renewal terms are non-compliant and must be flagged so
the requester discloses the renewal terms before a purchase order is
generated.
